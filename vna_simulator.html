💡 What is S11 / Return Loss?

S11 represents the reflection coefficient (Input Return Loss). It measures how much radio frequency power is reflected back from the device. A value of -20 dB means only 1% of the power is reflected (great match), while 0 dB means 100% reflection (total mismatch or open/short).

Complete VNA Learning Handbook & Button Reference

1. What is a Vector Network Analyzer (VNA)?

A VNA is an instrument used to test RF components. It injects a swept radio frequency signal into a Device Under Test (DUT) and measures both the magnitude and phase of the reflected (S11) or transmitted (S21) waves.

2. Reference Guide to Interface Buttons

  • Log Mag Button: Displays return loss in decibels (dB). Allows engineers to easily see impedance match quality across a wide dynamic range (e.g., -10dB to -50dB).
  • Smith Chart Button: A graphical polar plot representing complex impedance ($R + jX$). The center point is a perfect 50Ω match. Moving right is inductive, moving left is capacitive.
  • Phase Button: Shows the phase angle of the reflected wave in degrees. Useful for measuring electrical delay and cable length.
  • VSWR Button: Voltage Standing Wave Ratio. Expresses the ratio of maximum to minimum voltage standing wave amplitude. A VSWR of 1.0 is ideal.
  • Perform SOL Cal: Simulates Short-Open-Load calibration standards to remove systematic cable errors and measurement noise.

3. Understanding DUT Behaviors

When you click different DUTs, notice how the trace shifts: Matched Load gives a flat low curve; Open/Short bounce up to 0 dB reflection; Filters show sharp frequency cutoffs; and Antennas display resonant impedance dips.

4. Importing Touchstone (.s1p) Files

You can click the Import .s1p button in the top header to load real-world 1-port Touchstone files. The VNA will automatically parse the frequency and complex reflection data ($S_{11}$ Real/Imaginary or Magnitude/Angle) and render it on your screen!
